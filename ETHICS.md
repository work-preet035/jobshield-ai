# Ethics Policy

JobShield AI is a defensive security tool. It exists to protect job seekers from fraud, not to surveil, profile, or harm anyone.

## Principles

**Defensive use only.** This tool detects scams to protect users. It must not be repurposed to identify legitimate recruiters as fraudulent, to defame individuals or companies, or to conduct offensive security operations.

**No PII retention.** Content submitted for analysis is stored only to show users their own scan history. It is not shared with third parties, not used to train models, and not linked to any user identity.

**Transparency.** Every detection decision is explainable. The rule engine is open source. Users can inspect exactly which pattern triggered a flag and why.

**False positive awareness.** No detection system is perfect. A high risk score is a signal to investigate further, not a verdict. Users are advised to verify findings independently.

**AI explanations are advisory.** The GPT-generated explanation is a second opinion from a language model, not a legal or security determination. Treat it as a starting point for your own research.
